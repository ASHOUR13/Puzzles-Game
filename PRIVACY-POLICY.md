# Puzzle Studio Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 14, 2026**

Puzzle Studio is an offline puzzle game. The app does not require an account and, in this provided build, does not include ads, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Photos

If you choose a photo for a puzzle, the app reads the image you select and processes it locally on your device. The photo is not uploaded or shared by Puzzle Studio. You can use the game without selecting a photo.

## Local storage

Puzzle Studio saves game progress, unlocked levels, earned stars, preferences, and best scores in local browser storage on your device. These records are not transmitted to us. Clearing app storage removes them.

## Data sharing and retention

This build does not transmit personal data to a server. Game progress and settings remain on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Photos are used in memory for the current game and are not saved as a photo library by the app.

## Children

Puzzle Studio has no account, chat, advertising, or social features in this build. Parents or guardians can contact the developer using the Play Store developer contact shown on the listing.

## Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Play Store Data safety answers must be updated before release. For privacy questions, use the developer contact information on the Puzzle Studio Google Play listing.

This document is a release template. Before publishing, replace the contact wording with a real support contact, publish this policy at a public, non-geofenced HTTPS URL, link that URL in Play Console, and verify that the policy matches the final app binary and every included SDK.
