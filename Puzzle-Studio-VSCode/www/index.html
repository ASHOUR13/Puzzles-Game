Privacy

Your puzzles stay on your device

This build does not require an account and does not include ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Game progress, stars, preferences, and scores are stored locally on this device.

If you choose a photo, Puzzle Studio processes it locally for the puzzle. It is not uploaded or shared by the app.

Clearing app storage removes saved scores. The app does not send personal data to a server.

For questions, contact the developer through the support contact shown on the app's Google Play listing.
